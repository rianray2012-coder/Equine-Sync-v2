# EquineSync — Week One Watchlist
_The final pre-launch artifact · what we are paying especially close attention to during the founder barn's first week_

> Not a QA checklist. Not a rollout tracker. Not an issue queue.
>
> Just a small set of things we want to watch carefully while real barn
> life encounters the platform for the first time. Each item is a place
> where the difference between "software that works" and "software that
> gets depended on" is most likely to reveal itself.
>
> Read this once on Sunday before week one begins. Then put it down and
> watch quietly. Capture what you see in `POST_LAUNCH_OBSERVATION_LOG.md`.

---

## 1 · Pre-dawn feed survives

The 04:55 to 06:30 AM window is the densest workload of the day and the moment when staff most need the platform to disappear into the background.

What we're watching for:

- Whether the groom opens Today on the way into the barn or after starting the round on muscle memory.
- Whether the filter chip ("Feed") is set once and forgotten — or re-tapped every reopen.
- Whether swipe-right registers cleanly with cold hands and gloves on.
- Whether anyone takes a photo of a paper feed chart "just in case."

If pre-dawn feed survives, the rest of the day usually does too.

---

## 2 · Turnout speed under pressure

Turnout is the workflow where bulk-mode and the Select-all chip were specifically designed. Week one tells us whether those affordances are discovered naturally or remain invisible.

What we're watching for:

- Bulk mode toggle adoption (does anyone use it, or is single-tap-per-row the default).
- Whether Select-all is found inside bulk mode without being shown.
- How long the full AM turnout round takes from first swipe to last.
- Whether a groom holding a lead rope can still register a completion one-handed.

---

## 3 · Interruption recovery moments

Every barn day has at least three: phone in pocket, signal drops, kid walks up, horse spooks, lid closes mid-form. We want to see what survives.

What we're watching for:

- Whether anyone notices the QuickAddSheet draft-restore (the design intent is that it should be invisible — they shouldn't notice it, they should just keep going).
- Whether the Today filter persists across phone-lock without complaint.
- Whether the inline "Saved as draft — try again when you have signal" appears under real signal loss, and what the user does after seeing it.
- Whether anyone restarts a form they'd already started because they thought it was lost.

---

## 4 · Owner digest engagement

The daily digest fires at 07:00 UTC. The weekly recap fires Sunday 18:00 UTC. We want to see how owners actually consume them.

What we're watching for:

- What time of day the first owner opens the email.
- Whether they reply, forward, archive, or read-silently.
- Whether anyone phones the barn after reading something specific in a digest.
- Whether owners ask about horses that are not their own.
- Whether the Sunday recap gets engagement that the daily digest doesn't.

---

## 5 · Notification trust

The Batch C bell drawer now surfaces pending owner requests with one-tap Approve / inline Decline. Week one tells us whether the latency drops as designed.

What we're watching for:

- Median time from owner request submission to admin decision.
- Whether admins approve faster than they decline, and whether the decline-note is filled in or left empty.
- Whether anyone uses the bell drawer at all, or still navigates to `/owner-portal` out of habit.
- Whether the badge count is glanced at frequently or ignored.

---

## 6 · Mobile usability outdoors and in motion

The walkways, the aisle, the back pasture, the trailer — these are where mobile-readiness actually gets tested.

What we're watching for:

- Where in the facility the platform is opened (aisle, tack room, office, parking lot).
- Whether anyone uses the platform while walking versus stationary.
- Whether one-handed operation holds while holding a lead rope, a feed bucket, a phone in cold weather.
- Whether glare on a sunny day creates legibility problems we haven't seen.
- Whether the FAB position works for left-handed users (we have not tested this; week one tells us).

---

## 7 · Whether Today becomes instinctive

The strongest possible signal in week one. Not what people say — what their thumb does on the home screen.

What we're watching for:

- Whether `/today` becomes the default tap, the way an email app is the default tap.
- Whether anyone has to be reminded where to start their day.
- Whether the Operational Pulse page is opened more than any other page.
- Whether anyone says, unprompted, "let me check what's left" and instinctively opens Today.

When Today becomes the default first tap of the day, the platform has begun to install itself into the operational rhythm of the barn.

---

## 8 · Where staff verbally coordinate instead

Voice coordination doesn't go away — nor should it. But we want to see where the platform should be carrying load and isn't.

What we're watching for:

- Topics that staff coordinate by voice or text that the platform technically supports.
- Topics that staff coordinate by voice or text that the platform _should_ support but doesn't yet (post-beta backlog candidates).
- Topics where voice coordination is genuinely the right channel (the platform should stay out of the way of these).
- Whether the trainer and the groom communicate through the platform at all, or whether they still use a separate channel.

---

## 9 · Whether the workflow feels lighter or heavier over repeated use

A platform that gets heavier with use is failing. A platform that gets lighter with use is working.

What we're watching for:

- Whether end-of-week feels easier than start-of-week for the staff (it should).
- Whether anyone has begun to develop personal shortcuts or favourites.
- Whether anyone has begun to trust the platform with something they would have written on paper at the start of the week.
- Whether the digest cadence feels right or wrong by Friday.

---

## What we are deliberately NOT watching

It is just as important to name what we are not doing this week:

- **No feature ideas mid-week.** Roadmap reactions wait for the three-week accumulation rule documented in `POST_LAUNCH_OBSERVATION_LOG.md`.
- **No tone changes mid-week.** If a copy line reads wrong on Tuesday, we write it down and revisit on Friday.
- **No performance optimization mid-week** unless something is materially blocking operations.
- **No celebration of single positive moments**, just as we are not panicking at single negative ones. Patterns matter, instances do not.

The point of week one is calm observation. Not validation, not iteration — observation.

---

## Read this on Sunday. Then close it.

After that, only `POST_LAUNCH_OBSERVATION_LOG.md` matters. This file is here to focus the watching, not to direct it.

If on Friday of week one you can fill in even three sections of the observation log with real behavioural entries — and you can write the third column of the monthly roll-up table honestly — week one has been a success regardless of what those entries say.

— End of week one watchlist.
